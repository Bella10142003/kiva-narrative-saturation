## 2. Proposed Analytical Approaches

### Outcome and comparison pools

The loan is the analysis unit. Funding duration is the fractional hours between fundraisingDate and raisedDate, modelled as log(1 + hours) to retain sub-day variation while limiting extreme-duration influence. Loans that never fund are excluded from headline models; we report their share and re-estimate as a censored accelerated failure-time model.

For loan i posted at t_i, the comparison pool is every same-sector loan posted in the prior fourteen days, whatever its later status, so the pool depends only on posting time. We prefer this to the listings still active at t_i, because slow loans linger there: a sector-week in which everything funds slowly yields both a larger active set and a slower focal loan, which week fixed effects cannot absorb. The active-set version is reported alongside. A posting-time pool measures the lender's actual choice with error, so its coefficients attenuate; we read magnitudes as lower bounds and interpret only associations agreeing in direction across both.

We report the distribution of pool size and require only a non-empty pool, because restricting to large pools removes the crowding variation that identifies the interaction; a ten-loan minimum is a robustness check.

### Measures and primary model

We measure the narrative environment in two windows, current and recent, and in each by volume, homogeneity, and their interaction. Saturation is the interaction: neither many loans nor similar stories is sufficient alone. Homogeneity is a mean, not a sum, so it does not rise mechanically with pool size. All six measures are centred and standardised.

| | Current pool (posted within 14 days before t_i) | Lagged pool (L to L+29 days before t_i) |
|---|---|---|
| Volume | C_i: log same-sector count | V_i: decayed count, any later status |
| Homogeneity | H_i: mean cosine to that pool | G_i: decayed mean cosine to that pool |
| Saturation | C_i x H_i | V_i x G_i |

The lagged window must begin after most loans have left the page, or recent and current alternatives are the same listings. Day 1 fixes L at the platform fundraising cap, which also makes the two pools non-overlapping by construction; the window runs to L+29 days with a seven-day decay half-life, following typical advertising carryover.

`log(1 + FundingHours_i) = b0 + b1*C_i + b2*H_i + b3*(C_i x H_i) + b4*V_i + b5*G_i + b6*(V_i x G_i) + g*X_i + Country FE + Activity FE + Week FE + e_i`

RQ1 tests the current interaction; RQ2 is the joint test that the three lagged terms are zero together, since correlation makes them individually uninterpretable. X_i includes the loan, borrower, repayment, and posting-time controls listed in Section 3, the valid disbursal-to-posting gap, and each description's repeated-boilerplate share. Standard errors are clustered by country and week. Coefficients are incremental conditional associations, not separately identified causal effects.

### Text representation

The browse view surfaces the short use statement; the full description is read only after click-through. We compute homogeneity on use, since appeals are hardest to tell apart at scan stage, and repeat it once on description as a consistency check. whySpecial enters only if Day 1 confirms coverage and variance. Text is cleaned and masked, then represented as L2-normalised word unigram and bigram TF-IDF vectors. Cosine similarity is transparent, outcome-independent, and scales through sparse rolling vector sums rather than an all-pairs matrix. Profiles are uploaded by Lending Partners and may be edited or translated (Kiva, n.d.), so under rules frozen before outcomes are viewed we identify high-frequency verbatim sequences and re-estimate on de-boilerplated text.

### Evolution, heterogeneity, and falsification

RQ3 traces each saturation term's annual marginal effect with confidence intervals. Splitting a main effect across ten years divides precision accordingly, so the trajectory is descriptive evidence on how the two channels moved, not a signed test.

RQ4 uses a joint interaction test before contrasts within the three largest sectors, with false-discovery-rate control. Subgroup estimates are far less precise, so we pre-define a 5% change in expected funding time as the smallest meaningful effect and report retransformed funding-time ratios; imprecise estimates are not evidence of absence.

The falsification test mirrors the lagged measures but shifts the window to L through L+29 days after t_i. Future listings cannot influence past funding, so any association there reflects unabsorbed language or time trends. Because a ratio of two indistinguishable coefficients is uninformative, the test runs only where the backward term is itself distinguishable from zero, and passes when the forward interval covers zero and a test of equality with it rejects.

Day 1 audits statuses, terminal dates, field variance, and pool coverage, then freezes L, the half-life, and the boilerplate rules. Day 2 builds vectors and both pools. Day 3 estimates headline and active-set models. Day 4 completes RQ3, RQ4, and falsification. Days 5-7 build the tool and presentation. The censored model and the description replication are dropped first if time is short.

## 相对 v4 的具体改动

| 改动 | v4 | 简化版 | 依据 |

| 主规格换成镜像池 | 主动池为主 | 前 14 天发布的池为主，主动池并列报告 | 主动池把 C 高估 3.2 倍；镜像池几乎无偏 |
